💧 About AquaTread

AquaTread is a small tool built by four engineering students who were tired of Google Maps sending them into flooded underpasses every monsoon. It picks a route that actually matches your vehicle's ground clearance.

  • Ride-aware routing: Scooters avoid water over 15cm, sedans over 30cm, SUVs over 45cm.
  • Fake-photo filter: We check the photo, the GPS tag, and the live rain radar before trusting a report.
  • Live detours: If a flood is on your path, we quietly recalculate and show you a safer way.
  • Voice guidance: Hands stay on the wheel, eyes stay on the road.
  • Finds the small stuff: Local shops, housing societies, and office towers show up in search.

Built by Team ThunderStrike — Hiya, Ayati, Srishti & Devansh (JSS Noida).

🛡️ Your Privacy

Your location stays on your phone. We don't log where you've been, and photo GPS tags are only used once, right when you submit a report, to confirm you were actually there.

📄 A quick note

Our detours are suggestions, not instructions. If you see a traffic barricade or an official flood warning, please listen to that first.
